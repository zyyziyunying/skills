# Style Patterns

These are editing examples, not authorship tests or banned-word rules. Apply only when the pattern weakens the supplied text; preserve its facts, voice, uncertainty, and useful structure.

### 14. Overused dashes

**Guidance:** Revise repeated dash interruptions when they create a formulaic rhythm. Preserve useful punctuation, number ranges, quotations, and the author's established voice. A dash is not evidence of AI authorship; a user-requested punctuation convention takes precedence. Periods, commas, colons, parentheses, or a rewritten sentence may improve a crowded sentence.

**Before:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions—not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address—yet this mislabeling continues—even in official documents.

**After:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions, not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address, yet this mislabeling continues in official documents.

**Before:**
> The new policy — announced without warning — affects thousands of workers. The changes -- long overdue according to critics -- will take effect immediately.

**After:**
> The new policy, announced without warning, affects thousands of workers. The changes, long overdue according to critics, will take effect immediately.

Review punctuation for readability, not a zero-dash quota.


### 15. Overuse of Boldface

**Problem:** AI chatbots emphasize phrases in boldface mechanically.

**Before:**
> It blends **OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)**, **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)**, and visual strategy tools such as the **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** and **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)**.

**After:**
> It blends OKRs, KPIs, and visual strategy tools like the Business Model Canvas and Balanced Scorecard.


### 16. Inline-Header Vertical Lists

**Problem:** AI outputs lists where items start with bolded headers followed by colons.

**Before:**
> - **User Experience:** The user experience has been significantly improved with a new interface.
> - **Performance:** Performance has been enhanced through optimized algorithms.
> - **Security:** Security has been strengthened with end-to-end encryption.

**After:**
> The update introduces a new interface, improves performance through optimized algorithms, and adds end-to-end encryption.


### 17. Title Case in Headings

**Problem:** AI chatbots capitalize all main words in headings.

**Before:**
> ## Strategic Negotiations And Global Partnerships

**After:**
> ## Strategic negotiations and global partnerships


### 18. Emojis

**Problem:** AI chatbots often decorate headings or bullet points with emojis.

**Before:**
> 🚀 **Launch Phase:** The product launches in Q3
> 💡 **Key Insight:** Users prefer simplicity
> ✅ **Next Steps:** Schedule follow-up meeting

**After:**
> The product launches in Q3. Users prefer simplicity. The next step is to schedule a follow-up meeting.


### 19. Curly Quotation Marks

**Guidance:** Match the document's typography or the user's requested convention. Curly quotes are normal typesetting and do not need replacement merely to make text sound human.

**Before:**
> He said “the project is on track” but others disagreed.

**After:**
> He said "the project is on track" but others disagreed.
